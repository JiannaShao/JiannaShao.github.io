01 — Form

Planar Construction

Exploring form, structure, and space through paper.

Medium
Paper
Focus
Fluid motion through solid form

1Design statement

Expressing fluidity through solid form by reimagining waves with a strong yet dynamic material.

2Sketch models

The five images above were all sketch models that used different techniques to mimic a liquid motion. Ultimately the bottom right sketch model was chosen as the basis of our development, as its boundaries mimicked a shell and its internal forms embodied the movement of the ocean the most.

Sketch model Final

3Final model

The final shape is a complex axis, containing twisted and curved planes. The curve of the main shape and the folds of the strips are designed to crash downward, mimicking the collapse of real waves inwards on top of themselves in the ocean.

02 — Application

Sound Vessel

Exploring the full range of designing, developing and producing a product.

Tools
Onshape · Blender · Bambu Studio
Materials
Plastilina · PLA

1Design statement

The exploration of resonating sound inspired by natural processes, culminating in a piece with a mind of its own.

2Concepts

Designing various sound vessels

This is a CAD model I created in Onshape for a percussion instrument inspired by the cello and frog guiros that was not ultimately picked to be developed. The chamber-like shape allows for the sound to project, while the interaction between the stick and the textures in the instrument creates different sounds. Hover the model to take it apart.Tap the model to take it apart.

3Development

Production

The design ultimately chosen was a modern, slightly neo-gothic version of the ancient Aztec death whistle. The original sketch model was made with plastilina, with the concavity in its side serving as the chamber for sound to resonate. Ultimately the modeling was done in Blender in order to achieve the "sculpted" texture desired in the product, and Bambu Studio was the slicing software used for 3D printing in PLA.
