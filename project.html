01

Game Design Projects

A collection of illustrated assets and animated sprites for Sunpuddle, a Construct3-made visual-focused game project done solo.

Part I

Sunpuddle

RoleSolo illustrator and developer

ToolsConstruct3, Clip Studio Paint

TeamSolo

StatusRetired before release

Context

Sunpuddle was my first solo endeavor when it came to game design, and I imagined a cozy farming game (a bit like Stardew Valley) but with more character interaction features and a painted storybook feeling. The assets were drawn in CSP while the game engine used was Construct3 due to its superior handling of graphics and manipulating visual effects.

Process

Since it was an open world, lots of assets were needed to fill all the space and the different items and furniture had to be interactable too! From dandelion plants that released seeds, to crop planting mechanisms and fishing minigames everything had to be individually painted and coded into the game.

Drag the magnets around

Takeaway

Sunpuddle taught me how much an open-world game asks of a solo developer, and how fast my own art could grow. My illustration style improved so much over the project that finishing it cohesively would have meant redrawing most of the early assets, on top of a time commitment I couldn't keep up with as a student. I chose to retire it, and I now scope projects with that in mind: a smaller world, a fixed art style, and a clear finish line.

Part II

Sketchy Secret

A promotional video, character animation, and gameplay scenes developed for Sketchy Secret, a Godot-based collaborative project done with Nadine McMahon (a student at USC).

ToolsGodot

TeamWith Nadine McMahon (USC)

StatusLevel 1 of 3 complete and tested

Context

Sketchy Secret is a mystery game that follows the detective Bri as she looks for the missing artists. It turns out that they are trapped inside their own artwork, and she has to explore the worlds they've imagined in order to find the root of the problems that trapped them in the first place.

Outcome

The first level (out of three) was completed and tested, but the rest of the game was put on hold when my game development partner and I went to different universities. We hope to pick it up and finish it someday.

03

Vector Ventures

A MATLAB-based educational game combining generated maps, math problems, and a text-driven adventure.

ToolsMATLAB

CourseIntro to MATLAB final project

Levels3, from 2D maps to a 3D map

MathVectors, dot product, cross product

Context

Intro to MATLAB Final Project

This began as the final project of my introductory MATLAB course in university, and eventually also became a study tool for myself when preparing for my Multivariable Calculus final exam. It is designed to have 3 levels that progress in difficulty, featuring different maps and math problems.

Level 01

Learning the Gameplay

This is essentially the tutorial level, featuring very light math but mainly adjusting the player to the text based format and the problem solving system.

Level 02

Actual Vector Problems

A new map is generated here for players to start applying what they've learned. Players are introduced to more complex concepts, such as the dot product.

Level 03

Advanced Problems

Finally, players are advanced to a 3D map and given vectors with x,y and z-values. They're now recalling concepts from earlier in the 2D space and working with concepts like cross product.
